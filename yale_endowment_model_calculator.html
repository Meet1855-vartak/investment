Institutional Methodology & Swensen Principles

1. Equity Bias & Illiquidity Premium: The Institutional Yale Model allocates heavily to private equity, venture capital, and real assets where active management dispersion and illiquidity premiums historically generate superior real compounding over multi-decade horizons. 2. Hybrid Spending Rule: To prevent dramatic lifestyle or budgetary cuts during equity bear markets while still capturing long-term growth, Yale calculates annual distributions as a weighted blend (typically 80%) of the prior year's inflation-adjusted distribution plus (20%) of the target spending rate applied to current endowment market value. 3. Benchmark Comparison: Compared against a traditional 60% Global Equities / 40% Aggregate Bonds portfolio (7.2% nominal expected return, 10.5% volatility) subject to the exact same spending rule and inflation assumptions.
